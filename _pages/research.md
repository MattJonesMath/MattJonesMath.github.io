**Research Interests**

My research interests are broad and interdisciplinary, but center around using mathematical tools to study applied problems. These tools include probability, game theory, data science, algorithms, graph theory, and statistics. I'm interested in problems from many different domains, including network science, social choice and the mathematics of democracy, quantitative social science, mathematical modeling, and human behavior.
